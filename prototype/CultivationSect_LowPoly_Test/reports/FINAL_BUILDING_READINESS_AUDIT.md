### Medicine integration contract, verified still present

The camera independently queries layer 1. This keeps smooth ramp walking while blocking the camera on stair/herb detail. Mask 0 alone does not make the body camera-only for every other actor; production integration must carry this exception behavior or define appropriate separate camera layers.
